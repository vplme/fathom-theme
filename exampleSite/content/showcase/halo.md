Meridian came to us with a banking app that had grown a tab for every department and a user base that opened none of them. The brief was a full rebuild in a quarter.

We cut the product down to one screen that mattered — a spending timeline that never lies about balances, pending charges or fees — and rebuilt everything else in service of it.

The app shipped on time, support tickets halved within two months, and the timeline is now the pattern the bank designs every new feature around.
