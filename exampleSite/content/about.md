Fathom is a three-person studio working from a single workshop. We take on a
handful of projects a year and do the design, engineering and motion ourselves —
no handoffs, no account layer.

We work in short, focused engagements: a clear brief, a small team, and
something real shipped at the end of it. If that sounds like what you need,
write to hello@fathom.studio.
