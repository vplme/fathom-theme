Twelve weeks for a banking app is not enough time, which is exactly why the constraint was useful: it made every scope conversation short and every decision cheap to reverse.

We shipped one screen — the spending timeline — and made it genuinely good before touching anything else. Everything the app does now hangs off that one honest view.

The rebuild came in on time because we never argued about the roadmap; we argued about the timeline screen. Pick the one thing that matters and let it drag the rest into shape.
