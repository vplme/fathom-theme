---
Field Notes Co made beautiful notebooks that nobody wanted to write in. The brand was so precious it felt like a crime to open one. Our brief, unofficially, was to give people permission.

We swapped the ornate identity for something plainer and more confident — a single grotesque, generous margins, and photography of notebooks that were already full of scribbles.

Sales are up, but the metric I care about is the reorder rate: people are finishing them now, and coming back for more. A notebook is only precious once it is used.
