title: "Animating with restraint"
date: 2026-04-09
category: "Craft"
author: "nadia"
description: "The best motion is the kind you never notice. A short argument for doing less, slower."
---
A good transition answers a question the eye was already asking: where did this come from, where did it go. Everything beyond that is decoration, and decoration in motion reads as noise.

I spend most of my time removing frames, not adding them. A move that felt great at 600ms usually feels right at 240, once the ego of the animation has been trimmed away.

Restraint is not the absence of craft. It is the craft — knowing exactly how little will do the job, and having the nerve to ship only that.
