---
title: "How we build without a design system"
date: 2026-05-28
category: "Process"
author: "lior"
description: "For a team of three, a full design system is overhead we cannot afford. Here is what we do instead."
---
A design system is a fixed cost. For a large org it pays for itself; for three people it is a museum you have to dust. We skip it, and we ship faster for it.

Instead we keep a short set of decisions — a type scale, a spacing rhythm, a handful of colours — written down where everyone can see them, and we make them again from scratch on each project so they stay honest.

When something repeats four times, it becomes a component. Before that, it is just markup, and markup is cheap to change. The discipline is knowing which is which.
