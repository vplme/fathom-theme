---
Most software is loud. It buzzes, badges and nudges, all in the name of engagement — and most of it earns the opposite: a quiet resentment that builds until the app is deleted.

Calm software does less on purpose. It surfaces what matters now, hides what can wait, and trusts the person using it to come back when they need to. That trust is the whole product.

The hard part is not the restraint itself but defending it, meeting after meeting, against every reasonable-sounding request to add just one more thing. Calm is a position you hold, not a style you apply.
